# Risk Register

Track risks early. A senior project makes uncertainty visible.

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Mitigation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Low/Medium/High | Low/Medium/High |  |  | Open |

## Risk Categories

- Product risk
- Technical risk
- Security risk
- Data risk
- Operational risk
- Delivery risk
- Dependency risk

## Review Cadence

Review risks:

- Before build starts.
- Before major architecture decisions.
- Before release.
- After incidents.
